Support

LineGuard is an iPhone app for care reminders and education about lines, tubes, drains, and similar devices. It is not medical advice, and it does not contact your clinic.

Contact

Email info@gatherwith.app. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, and what you expected to happen. Do not send photos of discharge paperwork or medical records.

LineGuard Plus

LineGuard Plus is optional. After a 7-day free trial, it is $7.99 per month or $49.99 per year. Apple shows the price for your region in the App Store.

  • Payment is charged to your Apple ID.
  • The subscription renews unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends.
  • Cancel or manage it in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
  • If you reinstall the app, use Restore purchases in Profile.
  • Call 911 still works if you are not subscribed.

Terms of Use: Apple Standard EULA

Reminders

If reminders do not appear, check Profile → Notification settings, then iPhone Settings → Notifications → LineGuard. The Home Screen widget shows task names on this phone. It does not send them to LineGuard.

Privacy

Read the Privacy Policy. Care details, photos, and your care-team number stay on this iPhone. LineGuard does not upload them.
